● Listening
Hold Super + J, speak, and let go.

Your voice, typed

A GNOME extension provides the shortcut and types the text; a background service handles the audio and Whisper.

Dictation

Hold the shortcut while you speak and let go: the text is typed into VS Code, your browser, the terminal or any document.

Super + J · a quick tap = hands-free

Live transcription

Meetings, classes or videos: records your microphone and your computer’s audio at the same time and writes a .txt labelled “Yo” (you) and “Otros” (others).

Super + Shift + J

Files

Turns an audio or video file into a .txt next to the original, with a timestamp for every sentence.

dictado transcribe clase.mp4

Spanish and English

Detects the language of each sentence by itself, with accents, ñ and punctuation.

No echo

With speakers, your microphone also hears the others: Dictado recognizes that echo and does not write it down twice.

D-Bus API

Other extensions can add a microphone button or show your voice level without bundling any audio or Whisper code.

Install

One command downloads the latest release, checks its fingerprint, and installs it for your user. No sudo.

curl -fsSL https://jdswe.github.io/dictado/install.sh | bash

Getting started

  1. If Ubuntu lacks python3-venv: sudo apt install python3-venv ffmpeg
  2. Run the command above. It downloads the models once (about 350 MB).
  3. Log out and back in: a microphone appears in the top bar.

From source

  1. git clone https://github.com/jdswe/dictado.git
  2. cd dictado && ./install.sh
  3. To remove it: ./uninstall.sh (your texts are kept).

It knows who is speaking

No voice guessing: what comes in through your microphone is you, and what plays on your computer is everyone else. Each line is saved to disk as soon as it is transcribed, so nothing is lost if something closes.

While recording, the top bar shows a red REC with the elapsed time.

Questions

Why a GNOME extension?

On Wayland, a normal app cannot type into another one. The extension runs inside GNOME: it delivers the text through the input method (like the on-screen keyboard) and, when an app does not support that, pastes it through the clipboard and then restores what you had copied. No root, no /dev/uinput and no permission dialogs.

I press Super+J and only a “j” is typed

GNOME has not loaded the extension yet: log out and back in once after installing.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes, in Extensions → Dictado → Settings. Super+H is left for minimizing windows, as in GNOME.

Can it be faster?

In ~/.config/dictado/config.toml set dictation_model = "base" (slightly less accurate) or, if you only speak one language, languages = ["es"].

Does it work with Macify?

They are separate projects. Macify can add a microphone button that talks to Dictado over D-Bus.

Privacy

Your voice never leaves your computer.

Everything local

Audio is processed on your computer and never saved: only the text is. No account, no analytics, no server.

Consent

Live transcription records other people. Tell them and ask for permission: in many places it is required by law.

One download

It only goes online to fetch the Whisper models from Hugging Face, once.

This website

Sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It reads the release list from GitHub’s public API.
